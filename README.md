# Prediction of Functional Change Post Stroke Rehabilitation 
We will investigate whether adding patients with other diagnoses to a training dataset helps predict functional change in stroke patients. We will use the public San Raffaele rehabilitation records collected in Rome from 2015 to 2018. Our target will be the difference between discharge and admission Barthel Index scores, including improvement, no change and deterioration. The main experiment will compare stroke-only training with mixed-diagnosis training on the same held-out stroke patients. A secondary experiment will reuse these models on a wider held-out population. We will use individual admission activity scores and eligible demographic and clinical assessments, and compare linear regression, Ridge regression and Random Forest with a small parameter search. Mean absolute error in Barthel points will be our main measure, supported by uncertainty intervals and subgroup results. We expect the project to show whether additional diagnostic groups help or hinder prediction for stroke patients. The contribution will be a controlled population comparison and a reproducible workflow, with the limits of single-hospital data and internal validation clearly reported.
